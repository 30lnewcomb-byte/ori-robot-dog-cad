==========================================================================
ORI ARM - WRIST/GRIPPER LOAD ANALYSIS  (frozen baseline geometry)
==========================================================================

PETG density assumed      : 1270.00 g/cm^3
Gravity                   : 9.81 m/s^2
Safety margin (torque)    : 2.0x
HTD-45H locked torque     : 4.41 N.m (VERIFIED)
HTD-45H est. continuous    : 2.43 N.m (~55% locked, bus-servo practice)

REAL PART MASSES (from CAD volume, PETG):
  yaw_housing      152.5 g
  upper_arm        101.3 g
  forearm          197.5 g
  gripper           19.5 g
  TOTAL arm (PETG)   470.8 g
  + 6x HTD-45H servos     384.0 g  (current design only)
  => current arm all-up    854.8 g

PAYLOAD / TOOL ASSUMPTIONS:
  Target payload          : 500.0 g  (user spec)
  Payload CG              : at gripper tip plane, centred, fully extended
  Current gripper mass     : 19.5 g (includes its HTD-45H actuator)
  Passive-gripper target   : <= 80 g (no actuator/motor/battery/controller)

GEOMETRY / MOMENT ARMS (worst case = arm horizontal, fully extended):
  shoulder->elbow (L1)     : 70.0 mm
  elbow->wrist  (L2)       : 60.0 mm
  wrist->tool   (Lw)       : 28.0 mm
  reach to wrist joint     : 188.0 mm
  reach to tool CG/tip     : 216.0 mm

STATIC PAYLOAD TORQUE (fully extended, horizontal, gravity):
  Shoulder/Elbow hold payload : 1.059 N.m  (lever 216.0 mm)
  Wrist ROLL/PITCH hold payload: 0.137 N.m  (lever 28.0 mm)
  Wrist holds tool self-wt    : 0.003 N.m  (tool 19g, lever 14 mm)

GRIPPER ACTUATION (closing) REQUIREMENT:
  Required grip force (mu=0.4) : 12.26 N  for 500.0 g payload
  Jaw pivot->pad lever      : 15.0 mm
  Closing torque at jaw pivot: 0.184 N.m  (BEFORE transmission)
  With 2.0x margin       : 0.368 N.m

HTD-45H vs REQUIREMENTS:
  Shoulder/Elbow req 1.059 N.m  vs locked 4.41 / cont 2.43 -> margin 2.3x
  Wrist pose req     0.137 N.m  vs cont 2.43 -> margin 17.7x
  Gripper close req  0.368 N.m (margined) vs cont 2.43 -> OK

CONCLUSION (see Documentation/ARM_WRIST_ANALYSIS.md for full deliverables):
  - Wrist actuators are NOT torque-limited by the 500 g payload (req 0.137 N.m << cont).
  - Gripper closing torque is tiny (0.184 N.m); a SMALL servo or a
    passive transmission from a wrist servo easily covers it.
  - HTD-45H at wrist/gripper is OVERSIZED for torque; mass savings possible.
  - Recommendation: servo-IN-WRIST drives passive gripper via a light
    transmission (printed gear or shaft+linkage). Passive gripper <=80 g.
