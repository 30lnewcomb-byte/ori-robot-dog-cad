THESE .step FILES ARE THE AUTHORITATIVE, MANUFACTURABLE ORI GEOMETRY.
They are the source of truth for printing/machining.

The OpenSCAD/ folder in this package is a separate, simpler, AI-readable VIEW
model for looking at and discussing Ori on GitHub/ChatGPT. It is NOT the
manufactured geometry. Use CAD_STEP/*.step for the real parts.
The matching .stl print files are in CAD_STL/.
